\chapter{Examples}
\pgst{tfemch}

\label{ch:manyexamples}

\def\chaptertitle{Examples}
\def\headdate{23rd June 2024}

In this chapter various additional examples are given of the possibilities of
the core and add-ons of \tfem. The examples are clustered into sections that
either have a common equation/model or a common numerical subject. The actual
location of the example in the \tfem\ tree does not necessarily correspond to
the clustering into sections.
